Textile & Craft Legacy

Preserving local materials with contemporary silhouettes.

Bangladesh possesses one of the world’s most venerable textile histories. OneCart highlights both traditional mastery and emerging modern ateliers: fine count cottons, heritage handspun weaves, durable selvedge denim from regional mills, and supple locally cured leathers.
